SEAPORT HUBS

Our Strategic Export Seaports in India

Dispatches routed through specialized maritime gateways to minimize transit times and inland hauling:

Western Corridor Port Code: INNSA

Nhava Sheva (JNPT) — Mumbai

India’s largest container hub. Handles agricultural commodities, rice, engineering handicrafts, and processed foods with direct line sailings to Europe, Mediterranean, and North America.

Primary Exports: Rice, Spices, Guntur Chillies, Handicrafts
Turnaround: 24-48 Hours Container Gate-In
North-West Seaboard Port Code: INMUN

Mundra Port — Gujarat

Ultra-modern deep-water container port capable of handling ultra-large container vessels (ULCV). Offers the shortest sailing transit time to Gulf ports (Jebel Ali / Dammam).

Primary Exports: Agri Commodities, Spices, Brass Items
Sailing Time: 3-4 Days to Middle East Hubs
Eastern Gateway Port Code: INMAA

Chennai Port & Kattupalli

Direct eastern seaboard maritime link. Primary dispatch terminal for Thanjavur Ponni rice, traditional rices, millets, organic cotton fabrics, and Tirupur textiles to Southeast Asia and Australia.

Primary Exports: Ponni Rice, Textiles, Garments, Bronze Items
Direct Links: Singapore, Port Klang, Melbourne
South-West Malabar Port Code: INCOK

Cochin Port (ICTT Vallarpadam)

Located directly adjacent to international east-west sea lanes. Gateway for Tellicherry black pepper, Alleppey green cardamom, cold-pressed virgin coconut oil, and coir products.

Primary Exports: Black Pepper, Cardamom, Coconut Oil, Coir Mats
Feeder Transit: Colombo Transshipment in 24h
Southern Coromandel Port Code: INTUT

Tuticorin (V.O. Chidambaranar Port)

World’s leading export harbor for compressed coco peat blocks, grow bags, coir fibre bales, and coconut shell products sourced from the dense Pollachi-Tenkasi agricultural clusters.

Primary Exports: Coco Peat 5kg Blocks, Grow Bags, Coir Twine
Volume: Over 1,200 Containers Handled Monthly
Express Air Freight BOM / MAA / BLR / DEL

Air Cargo Priority Hubs

Temperature-controlled pharmaceutical & high-value commodity dispatch for green cardamom jumbo pods, Swamimalai bronze masterpieces, and emergency retail sample shipments.

Turnaround: 24-48 Hour Global Delivery via Emirates / Qatar Cargo
SLA: Priority Customs Clearance Under ATA Carnet / EDI
