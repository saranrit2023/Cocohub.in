ASSAY PROTOCOLS

Multi-Stage Quality Control Protocol

Zero-compromise testing at origin before cargo is cleared for ocean dispatch:

01

Farm Gate Assay

Raw material sampling directly from farmer cooperatives, testing soil heavy metal residues and moisture levels.

02

Optical Sortex Cleaning

Buhler Sortex optical sorting for rice and spices to eliminate foreign matter, discolored grains, and stones (99.9% purity).

03

NABL Lab Testing

HPLC assays for active curcumin, piperine, moisture % analysis, microbial bio-burden, and pesticide screenings.

04

Fumigation & ISPM-15

Methyl bromide or phosphine quarantine fumigation with government phytosanitary clearance certificate issued per shipment.

05

Digital Port Sealing

Container stuffing supervised by third-party surveyors with photographic timestamped proof before customs bolt sealing.
